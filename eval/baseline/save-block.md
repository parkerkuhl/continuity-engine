BLOCK 1, PLAYER SAVE

Premise: Marro, a sixteen-year-old squire, serves Rickard Snow, a sellsword captain of the Golden Company besieging Volantis. Debts, secrets and a traitor in the camp.
Setting: original low-fantasy Free Cities on the Rhoyne (no setting files). Genre and tone: grim low fantasy. Era and place: spring, year 300, the siege camp outside Volantis. Current moment for the era rule: the start of chapter 2, spring of year 300. Canon tracking: original world.
Voice anchor: clipped, close third person, past tense; plain diction, hard edges, dry humor; violence quick and ugly. Approved sample: "Rain came off the Black Wall in sheets, and the squire counted coins he didn't have." Interior voice: deep. Avoid: triplet lists, neat closing lines, "not X but Y", stock phrasing, tidy epigrams, NPCs who sound like the narrator. Voice rulings: none.
Difficulty: balanced. Length: standard. Dice: GM rolls. Turn length: standard. Lines: harm to children. Veils: sexual content.

Story so far:
- Chapter 1, The Black Wall: Rickard took a crossbow bolt in the left shoulder. Brother Olyvar, the company septon, died of fever and was buried outside the Black Wall. Marro broke two fingers of his right hand in a dice-house brawl. In the alleys Marro killed Dagna the smuggler in self-defense; her body went into the Rhoyne, and his dagger went with it. Hesse lost her left eye to the Triarch's men. The company's grain now comes on Triarch Malaquo's credit.
Current chapter and act: chapter 2, Act I.

Character sheet: Marro, 16, squire to Rickard Snow. Might 0, Finesse 2, Wits 2, Presence 1, Resolve 3. Health 6/6, Stress 1/5. Flaw: gambles when cornered. Secret: born a slave in Volantis, escaped as a child (only Hesse knows). Two fingers of his right hand broken and splinted: he can't grip a blade properly in that hand.
Inventory: a cudgel (his dagger was lost in the Rhoyne; he has no dagger), Rickard's old whetstone (a gift from Rickard), Lissa's letter, 4 silver stags, a borrowed cloak.
Trackers: debt to Pallo the moneylender, 30 silver stags, due at the next new moon (four days away). Bond (Hesse): trusted.

NPCs and companions:
- Rickard Snow: captain, Marro's master. Tall, grey-eyed, burn scar on the jaw. Wounded left shoulder: can't raise a shield or draw a bow with that arm, fights at -1. Wants land of his own. Promised Marro a knighthood if the company takes the city. Marro knows Rickard's mother was a daughter of House Maegyr, of the Old Blood (Rickard told him; no one else in the company knows). Speech: terse, sardonic.
- Sergeant Hesse: company sergeant, one-eyed (lost her left eye; wears a patch). Her horse, Ash, is lame; the company has no spare horses. Knows Marro's slave past. Marro swore to her that he would never gamble again. Speech: blunt, soldier's oaths.
- Lysono Qhoran: the company paymaster. Watches the captains' books and loyalties. Speech: soft, precise.
- Pallo: Volantene moneylender whose men dress in grey. Marro owes him 30 silver stags.
- Ser Tobbin: hedge knight in the company's pay, Dagna's brother. Doesn't know Dagna is dead or who killed her.
- Lissa: a ferry-girl. Marro promised to carry her letter to her mother in the Dragon's Den district.
- Triarch Malaquo: one of the three Triarchs of Volantis. The company owes him two months of grain, bought on credit.
- Dead: Brother Olyvar (fever, buried outside the Black Wall); Dagna the smuggler (killed by Marro; only Marro knows).

Open threads: Pallo's debt (due at the new moon); Lissa's letter; Rickard's promised knighthood; who in the company is selling its plans to the Triarch.

Setting notes: Volantis is split by the Black Wall; the Old Blood live inside it. The Long Bridge joins the two halves of the city and is closed by the Triarch's order: crossing needs a stamped pass, and Marro has none. The Dragon's Den is a district of eastern Volantis. Oath: "By the Black Wall."

Rulings: "Marro can read; his mother taught him."
