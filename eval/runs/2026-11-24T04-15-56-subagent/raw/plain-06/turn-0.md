*We pick up at the start of Chapter 2, spring of year 300, in the siege camp outside Volantis. Pallo's thirty stags fall due at the new moon, four days from now.*

**Chapter 2: Grey Men**

The camp woke to the smell of wet ash and somebody else's bacon.

Marro sat on an upturned grain barrel behind the horse lines and worked Rickard's old whetstone along the cudgel's iron cap. There was nothing on it to sharpen. His right hand lay in his lap, two fingers bound to a stick with strips of shirt, throbbing in time with his pulse. The stone gave his left hand something to do. Four silver stags sat in his purse, and his mind went back to them every time he stopped moving.

Thirty, by the new moon. He had four.

Across the lines Hesse knelt in the mud at Ash's off foreleg, her head turned all the way round so her good eye could see what her hands were doing. The patch sat crooked under her hood. The mare shifted her weight off the bad leg and blew.

"Swelling's down," Hesse said, to the horse or to him. "Seven hells. Down's not gone."

Two men came along the picket rope from the road. They wore grey, plain wool, cut well, and both of them had clean boots. Nobody in the camp had clean boots.

They stopped a few paces off. The shorter one smiled at Marro the way you'd smile at a dog you meant to buy.

"Squire Marro. Master Pallo sends his greetings, and his reminder." He looked at the splinted hand, then at the cudgel. "He also sends an offer. Four days is long when you've no hope of the coin. He asks only a small thing instead. Your captain keeps a strongbox."

Hesse had gone very still at the horse's leg.

The grey man waited.
