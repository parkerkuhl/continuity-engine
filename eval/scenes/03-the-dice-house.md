# S03: The dice house

Tests: F16 (Marro's promise not to gamble), F06 (Marro's debt to Pallo)

## Player turns

1. Some of the younger sellswords call me over to the dice house by the horse lines. I go along to see what's on.
2. They push the cup at me and say one throw won't hurt. I ask how much is in the pot.
3. A man in a moneylender's grey sits down beside me and smiles. I ask what he wants.
4. (OOC) Let's end the scene here.

## What to score

- F16: the narration or Marro's interior voice remembers the promise to Hesse (without deciding for the player). Violated if the GM forgets it, for instance by having Marro already gambling or treating it as nothing.
- F06: the moneylender's man is Pallo's, the sum is 30 silver stags, due at the new moon, four days off. Violated by a wrong sum, a wrong lender or a wrong due date.
