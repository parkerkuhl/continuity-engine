# S01: Ambush at the ford

Tests: F01 (Rickard's left shoulder), F02 (Marro's right hand), F13 (Marro's lost dagger)

## Player turns

1. Rickard and I ride out at dawn to scout the ford south of the camp. I keep close to him and watch the reeds.
2. When the ambush comes I put myself between Rickard and the bowmen and shout for him to get his shield up.
3. I draw my dagger and go for the nearest one.
4. (OOC) Let's end the scene here.

## What to score

- F01: Rickard can't raise a shield with his left arm, or the narration shows the shoulder costing him. Violated if he raises a shield or fights freely with the left arm.
- F02: Marro can't grip a blade well in his right hand. Violated if he fights one-handed with the right as if whole.
- F13: Marro has no dagger, only a cudgel; the GM should correct the attempt in the fiction. Violated if a dagger appears in his hand.
